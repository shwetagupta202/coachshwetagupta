# AI Edge Builder

Build a premium, modern personal brand website for an AI Agents Coach and Product Leader. This is a portfolio + authority site meant to convert visitors into workshop/coaching leads — not a generic resume site.

Who this is for

Shweta Gupta — AI Agents Coach | PLM Senior Technical Lead | Product Leader | Amazon Bestselling Author | Speaker | Community Builder. Based in Maharashtra, India. 15+ years in enterprise tech (Cisco, Huawei, Technia AB Sweden, Addnode India, Tata Technologies), now teaching working professionals to build AI agents, voice agents, and automations using no-code tools like n8n, Bolna AI, and ChatGPT. She has delivered 300+ live workshops, trained 5,000+ professionals, and runs an active coaching community.

Visual identity / theme

Palette: Dark, premium tech aesthetic — deep navy (#0B1220 to #0F1B2E range) as the primary background, with electric cyan (#22D3EE / #06B6D4) as the energetic accent and warm gold (#D4AF37 / #E8C468) for premium/credibility touches (awards, testimonials, CTAs). This mirrors the navy/cyan/gold theme already used across her coaching materials — keep it consistent.

Typography: A clean geometric sans-serif for headings (e.g. Space Grotesk, Sora, or Manrope) paired with a highly readable sans (Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans) for body text. Headings should feel confident and modern, not corporate-stiff.

Mood: Confident, credible, future-forward — "AI expert who's actually built enterprise systems," not "hypey AI influencer." Subtle glow/gradient accents on cyan elements, generous whitespace, dark-mode-first design.

Motion: Light, tasteful scroll-reveal animations and hover states on cards/buttons. Nothing gimmicky.
